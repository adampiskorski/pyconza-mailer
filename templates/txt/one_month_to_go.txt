Beginners day tutorial now available!
=====================================

Thanks to Sheena O'Connell, we will be hosting a free* beginners day tutorial, featuring the Humble Data Syllabus: https://humbledata.org/.
To attend, you will need to purchase a R50 refundable ticket here: https://za.pycon.org/pages/tickets/buy-tickets/.
This tutorial is designed for complete beginners in both Python and programming, particularly those who could not learn programming before.
Please book soon, as we have limited capacity!
